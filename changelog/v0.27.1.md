## What's New in v0.27.1

A small follow-up to v0.27.0 with two fixes.

### Fixed

- **Archived items are hidden from the working tree again, wherever they sit.** In v0.27.0, an archived epic nested under a milestone stayed visible under that milestone instead of moving to the Archived section. Archived items — epics under milestones, beads inside backlogged epics, loose beads in the grouped view — now all go to a single **Archived** section, collapsed by default; whether you have it open is one setting, remembered across workspaces and relaunches. Backlogged epics keep their v0.27.0 placement under their milestone; unarchiving puts an item back where it was.
- **Large workspaces: no more lost replies, and no more restart loop.** When several large replies from the background process arrived at once, one could be corrupted and never reach the app — the tree or a panel would sit on a spinner until something refetched. In v0.27.0 the new call deadline turned each lost reply into a restart, which on a very large workspace could repeat indefinitely, re-reading the whole workspace every time. Replies are now written one at a time, restarts are bounded, and a background process that is still answering other calls is no longer restarted for one slow one.

Requires the [beads](https://github.com/gastownhall/beads) CLI, version 1.1.0 or newer.
