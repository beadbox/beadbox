## What's New in v0.33.1

One fix.

### Fixed

- **On a busy board, the Beads tree no longer rebuilds every row on each live refresh.** A change that moved one row, such as a status change or a close, made the tree tear down and rebuild every row after it; that churn is what made the app's memory climb over a long session. Rows now keep their place across refreshes.

Requires the [beads](https://github.com/gastownhall/beads) CLI, version 1.1.0 or newer.
