## What's New in v0.34.1

A size on every row, and background windows that stay fresh.

### Added

- **Each bead row shows its size.** A compact XS, S, M or L badge sits where the estimate stopwatch used to be, so a long list reads at a glance. The size follows the bead's estimate in minutes (XS under 15, S under 60, M up to 180, L above), and a revised estimate recorded on the bead takes its place. A bead with no estimate shows a plain "?" instead of a guessed size, and the tooltip still gives the estimate itself.

### Fixed

- **A hidden window keeps refreshing, at a capped rate.** Since v0.34.0 a background window did not refresh at all; now the first change after opening a workspace or a quiet period refreshes at once, further changes coalesce into at most one tree refresh every 30 seconds, and returning to the window catches up immediately when changes are pending. Visible windows behave as before.

### Under the hood

- The packaged smoke test allows for the background refresh interval and fails if live updates do not arrive.

Requires the [beads](https://github.com/gastownhall/beads) CLI, version 1.1.0 or newer.
