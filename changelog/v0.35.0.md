## What's New in v0.35.0

The comment thread stays where you left it, the Workspaces sidebar takes your order, and live updates find their way back.

### Added

- **Reorder the Workspaces sidebar.** Drag a workspace to where you want it, or move it from the keyboard (Alt+Up and Alt+Down, Alt+Home and Alt+End, or Move up and Move down in the row menu); each move is announced to assistive technology. The order persists across restarts and updates, a new workspace goes to the end, and removing one keeps the rest in place.

### Fixed

- **The comment thread never moves on its own.** A new comment, whether yours or a live one, no longer scrolls the thread, and a comment that lands above what you are reading leaves your place exactly where it was. The jump-to-oldest pill is gone; your own scrolling, the keyboard and the minimap are unchanged.
- **Your edits survive a refresh.** Since v0.34.0, a tree read already in flight when you edited a bead could paint the old value over your change until the next read landed. Your edit now stays put.
- **Live updates recover on their own.** When a subscription fails to start, or the sidecar restarts under the app, the "Live updates paused" strip shows and the app keeps retrying, so updates come back without a relaunch. A page that was frozen by the system is no longer mistaken for a stalled stream, and one workspace never runs more than one change detector.

### Under the hood

- The packaged smoke test now opens the views a release changes inside the signed app and checks what they render, and the end-to-end specs no longer leak beads between each other.

Requires the [beads](https://github.com/gastownhall/beads) CLI, version 1.1.0 or newer.
