## What's New in v0.28.2

A fix for a hang in the Chart view, and four improvements to it.

### Fixed

- **The Chart could freeze on a dense dependency layout.** When six or more dependency arrows ran along the same gap between two rows, the arrow-placing step looped forever and the window stopped responding. It was in v0.28.0 and v0.28.1, most likely with **Links across epics** turned on. Arrows are now placed with a bounded search; in the rare case where a gap is full, two arrows may share a short horizontal stretch instead.
- **Arrows no longer take long detours.** With several epics stacked between two beads, an arrow could run far to the left past every bar before coming back. Arrows now cross the rows in between one at a time, stepping sideways only in the gaps between rows, so they stay close to the beads they connect. They still never cross a bar or share a vertical segment.

### Improved

- **The Chart keeps your place.** Switch to the Beads view and back within the same session and the Chart opens where you left it: the same zoom, the same time at the left edge, and the same vertical position. The window is remembered as a time, so beads added in the meantime don't shift it. A fresh app start opens the default view.
- **Pinch to zoom.** Pinch on the trackpad over the timeline and it zooms smoothly, keeping the moment under the pointer in place. The page itself no longer zooms. A pinch can leave the zoom between the preset steps; **+** and **−** then go to the next whole step.
- **One more zoom step.** **+** goes three steps past Hours instead of two, with the 15-minute labels, so short agent runs get more room.
- **No text selection while resizing.** Dragging the edge of the title column no longer selects bead titles as the pointer passes over them.
