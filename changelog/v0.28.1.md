## What's New in v0.28.1

Five improvements to the Chart view, from using it.

### Improved

- **Click a bar to open the bead.** Bars were the biggest targets on screen and did nothing when clicked; now they open the bead, like clicking its title. Epic bars stay as they were, and the title is still the keyboard path.
- **Resizable title column.** Drag the edge of the title column, or use ← and → on it, to widen it from 300 up to 600 px. The width is kept for the session.
- **Links across epics.** A new toggle next to the zoom controls draws dependencies between beads in different epics as arrows, routed through the gaps like in-epic arrows. A link into a collapsed epic attaches to that epic's header, and the matching dots are hidden while the toggle is on. It is off by default and remembered for the session.
- **Minute labels when zoomed in.** Past the Hours zoom, the axis labels every 30 minutes, then every 15 (`14:30`, `14:45`), so work that finishes within the hour is readable.
- **Zooming keeps now in view.** Every zoom change except Fit scrolls so the current time sits about three-quarters of the way across the view: recent work on the left, the near plan on the right. If now isn't on the timeline, zooming keeps the centre in place instead.
