## What's New in v0.33.0

All your projects in one table, each row still talking to its own workspace.

### Added

- **All projects view.** A new All projects tab lists every registered workspace's beads in one table. Each row belongs to one workspace: selecting, navigating dependencies and every read and write go to that row's own workspace, with no merged store and no export, so bd stays the source of truth. A workspace that cannot be reached does not hold up the others: its rows wait while the rest load, it is retried on its own, and Refresh tries them all again. The detail pane shows the row's workspace, and the Settings button is the gear icon alone, with a screen-reader label, there and in the workspace header.
- **Each row keeps its own workspace's status colors.** In All projects, every status in a row and in its detail pane is drawn in the colors you set for that workspace in Settings > Workflow; a workspace with no colors chosen looks exactly as it does on its own.

### From the community

Thanks to **[@izzipizzy](https://github.com/izzipizzy)** for the All projects view (#68).

### Under the hood

- The updater no longer pulls in the zip crate, and the Linux build is pinned to Ubuntu 24.04 with a check on the .deb's GLIBC ceiling.
- beadbox.app updates itself after every release, and a check turns red if the site lags the latest final.

Requires the [beads](https://github.com/gastownhall/beads) CLI, version 1.1.0 or newer.
