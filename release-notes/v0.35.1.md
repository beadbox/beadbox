## What's New in v0.35.1

One change.

### Changed

- **Archiving a bead from its row is instant.** The row leaves the list the moment you click Archive bead, without waiting for bd. If the archive fails, the bead comes back where it was and an error names it; a live refresh while the archive is still being written no longer brings the row back, and a second click makes no second archive.

Requires the [beads](https://github.com/gastownhall/beads) CLI, version 1.1.0 or newer.
