## What's New in v0.30.0

A new view, contributed by the community: see what your work is waiting on, and release it.

### Added

- **Waiting view for open gates.** bd gates, whether created with `bd gate create` or by a formula step that waits, hold work until a person, a timer, a GitHub run or pull request, or another bead releases it. Until now a held bead showed only as blocked, and finding out why meant `bd gate list` and `bd show` in a terminal. A **Waiting** tab (⌘6) now appears whenever the active workspace has an open gate, and disappears when none is left. It lists every open gate, oldest first, with its type, title and reason, how long it has been waiting, the deadline of a timer gate or the run, pull request or bead another gate awaits, and the open beads it holds: those blocked by the gate and their open descendants, the same way `bd ready` treats them. Each held bead opens in the detail panel. A human gate has a **Resolve** button with an optional reason; it runs `bd gate resolve`, and the app re-reads the gate first and refuses anything that is not an open human gate, so the app releases only human gates; timer, CI and bead gates are left to what they wait for. Gates created or resolved from the CLI show up live, and when the last one goes the view says "Nothing is waiting". Very large gate graphs are kept in bounds: a gate lists at most its first 200 held beads and the view at most 5,000, says so when it cuts a list, and points you at `bd dep list` for the rest.
