## What's New in v0.31.0

The Formulas tab draws nested formulas as the plan they are, molecules show their gates, due dates show their time, and the Beads view holds still.

### Added

- **A nested formula is drawn as its steps.** On the Formulas tab, each step of a nested formula is a group holding its children in dependency order, with edges between steps taken from what their children need and a closed/total count on each step (shown when a step has children; an empty step inside such a formula reads 0/0). Flat formulas draw as before, and the grouping works with no molecule selected. A step caught in a dependency cycle gets a "cycle" badge.
- **Active Molecules lists molecules poured on beads 1.3**, under any workspace id prefix. On 1.3 the list was always empty.
- **Molecules show their gates.** Each gate is a row in its step and phase, with its kind (human, timer, GitHub run or pull request) and its status. A timer gate shows when it expires. A closed gate counts as done.
- **Due dates show the time beside the date, in your time zone.** A date-only value shows the date alone. Within 24 hours a hint follows ("in 3 h", "in 40 min"), and once past, "overdue". The table, the detail panel and the expanded view all show the same text.

### Fixed

- **A molecule's phases in the Beads view no longer collapse and re-form** whenever anything in the workspace changes.
- **The epic row's progress bar is narrower** (96 px), and its count is never cut off.

### Under the hood

- The signed build now runs nightly and on demand instead of on every push to main, and every push to main gets its own complete check run.

Requires the [beads](https://github.com/gastownhall/beads) CLI, version 1.1.0 or newer.
