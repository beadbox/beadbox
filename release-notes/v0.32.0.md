## What's New in v0.32.0

Every status gets a color of your choosing, and a nested molecule reads as its plan in the Beads view.

### Added

- **A color for every bead status.** Settings > Workflow lists every status in the project, the built-ins and your custom ones, each with a color you can set. A chosen color applies at once, without a reload, everywhere Beadbox draws that status: badges in lists and the detail panel, pipeline tiles, grouped headers and the status filter. A custom status you have not colored gets a distinct default that is never grey and never one another status already uses. Reset puts every status back to its default; with nothing set, the built-ins look exactly as before. Text stays readable on any color you choose, and every control works from the keyboard. Colors are saved per workspace in Beadbox's own settings and survive a restart and a switch to another workspace and back; nothing is written to bd.
- **A nested molecule shows its steps in the Beads view.** A molecule poured from a formula with nested steps now shows one header per step, in the plan's order (a step is never drawn before a step it needs, whatever sort you use), with the step's children under it in dependency order and a closed/total count on each header. A step starts open until all its children are closed, then collapses; your own collapse or expand of a step sticks. The headers hold still when anything in the workspace changes.

### Under the hood

- macOS builds and the release pipeline now run on our own Mac runner, and the checks on a push to main are faster.

Requires the [beads](https://github.com/gastownhall/beads) CLI, version 1.1.0 or newer.
