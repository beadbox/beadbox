## What's New in v0.28.0

A Chart view: a dependency timeline for every epic.

### New

- **Chart view.** A new **Chart** tab (or **⌘5**) shows each epic as a timeline. Epics are collapsible sections with a summary bar, nested epics nest, loose beads sit under **Ungrouped**, and archived items are left out. It uses the same filters as the Beads view and updates live.
  - **What happened, and what's planned.** Left of the dashed **now** line is the record: each bead runs from when work on it started to when it closed, or to now while it is still running. Right of it is the plan: a bead that has not started is placed after everything that blocks it, for its estimated time (one hour when it has none), so a dependency chain reads in order and independent work stays side by side. The plan is worked out fresh each time; nothing about it is stored.
  - **Start times come from beads itself.** Beadbox reads the start time `bd` records when a bead first goes to in progress or is claimed. A bead that has none (moved straight to a custom status, or closed without ever being started) is placed after its blockers and drawn dotted and faded; the legend calls it **Start not recorded**. Opening the chart never changes your workspace.
  - **Dependencies you can follow.** Inside an epic, arrows run from the end of a bead to the start of the bead it blocks, through the gaps between bars. A dependency on a bead in another epic shows as a dot on the bar; hover over it or tab to it to see the other bead.
  - **Built for long lists.** Titles stay put while the timeline scrolls. Zoom between hours, days and weeks, fit everything on screen, or step in between with **+** and **−**. A legend explains every colour, outline, arrow and dot. Click a title to open the bead.

### Fixed

- **Live updates on local workspaces whose settings don't name a mode.** If a workspace's `metadata.json` had no mode setting and an old port file or registry entry pointed at a server, Beadbox kept polling it as a server workspace, `bd` refused every poll, and the list stopped updating until you clicked Refresh. Beadbox now recognises that refusal on the first poll and switches to watching the local store. A workspace registered against an external server that can't be reached still shows "Live updates paused", as before.
